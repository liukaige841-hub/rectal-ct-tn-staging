# 论文代码与数据可用性声明

## 中文声明

本研究最终模型的定义、训练与推理脚本、预处理及关键层/ROI 构建代码、固定配置和模型卡已公开，仓库地址为 [repository URL]。训练权重因未随代码公开，可在获得机构及伦理批准后根据合理申请提供。原始 CT 影像、分割掩膜及患者级数据涉及隐私与伦理限制，不公开；在符合适用法律法规、机构规定及伦理批准的前提下，可根据合理申请评估受控访问的可行性。

## English statement

The final model definitions, training and inference scripts, preprocessing and key-slice/ROI construction code, fixed configurations, and model card used in this study are publicly available at [repository URL]. The trained weights are not distributed with the code and may be made available upon reasonable request, subject to institutional and ethical approval. The raw CT images, segmentation masks, and patient-level data are not publicly available because of privacy and ethical restrictions. The feasibility of controlled access upon reasonable request may be assessed in accordance with applicable laws, institutional policies, and ethical approvals.
